This is an independent, fan-made front end for How Sad Is This Chapter?. It is not made, run, affiliated with or endorsed by that site or its creator.

All credit for the site, its idea and its design belongs to its creator, and every rating comes from readers on the original site. This app only reformats that public data for phones.
