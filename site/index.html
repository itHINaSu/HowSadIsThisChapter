About this app

A phone-friendly way to browse How Sad Is This Chapter?. Every rating comes from readers on that site. This is an unofficial companion, not run by the site's team.

To rate a book or add a new one, use the main site. Changes show up here after the next update.

Add it to your home screen from your browser's share or menu button to use it like an app.
